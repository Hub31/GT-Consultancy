Services

Every service below is grounded in work Godfrey has done directly — not a general capability list. Each links to the project evidence behind it.

Public Health Laboratory Systems Strengthening

When a national laboratory system fails, the cost isn't measured in equipment — it's measured in delayed diagnoses, unreliable data, and lost donor confidence. We build laboratory operations that hold up under real-world pressure, from the ground up.

Biorepository & Biobank Design and Management

Every sample in a biorepository represents a patient, a trial participant, or years of research that can never be recollected. We design biobank systems — cold-chain, tracking, international shipment — built so irreplaceable specimens never become a point of failure.

Disease Surveillance Systems

Outbreaks don't wait for paperwork. We strengthen the laboratory backbone of disease surveillance — for TB, HIV, and outbreak-prone diseases like Ebola — so case detection and reporting hold up when it matters most.

Laboratory Quality Systems & Accreditation Support

An ISO 15189 audit shouldn't feel like a threat. We install quality systems your team actually understands and owns, so accreditation becomes a byproduct of good daily practice — not a stressful annual scramble.

Biosafety & Biosecurity Risk Assessments

Biosafety failures don't make headlines until it's too late. We assess and strengthen the practical safeguards that protect your staff, your samples, and the surrounding community — grounded in frameworks including the WHO Laboratory Biosafety Manual.

Training & Capacity Building for Laboratory Teams

Consultants eventually leave. The systems and people they trained shouldn't need them to stay. We build training designed to make outside help unnecessary over time — real capacity, not just a certificate on the wall.

Laboratory Data Systems & Dashboard Design

A lab result is only as good as the data behind it. We close the gaps that quietly erode trust in laboratory data — the kind that don't surface until an audit, a funder review, or a patient outcome depends on it.

Technical Assistance to Ministries of Health, WHO-Aligned Partners, and Clinical Trial Sponsors

Ministries of health and global health partners don't need another report — they need someone who has actually run the lab, not just advised on it from a distance. That's the difference we bring to technical assistance engagements.

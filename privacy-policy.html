Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

GT Consultancy ("GT Consultancy", "we", "us") provides public health laboratory systems consulting. This policy explains what personal data this website (gtconsultancy.pro) collects, why, and what rights you have over it. It's written to be genuinely readable, not just legally defensible — if anything here is unclear, contact us using the details at the end and we'll explain it properly.

GT Consultancy is led by Godfrey Tuda Otieno and operates from Kilifi, Kenya (East & Central Africa office) and Freetown, Sierra Leone (West Africa office). For the purposes of this policy, and under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, GT Consultancy is the data controller for information collected through this website.

What we collect

This site collects personal data in one place only: the consultation request form on our Contact page. When you submit it, we receive whatever you enter in these fields:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Organization
  • Country
  • Project type (selected from a list)
  • Budget range (optional)
  • Your message

We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or advertising pixels anywhere on this site. We do not passively track your browsing behavior.

If you contact us via the WhatsApp link or QR code instead, that conversation happens on WhatsApp's own platform, governed by WhatsApp's Privacy Policy, not this one — we only see what you choose to send us there.

How we use it

We use consultation request details solely to:

  • Understand your project and assess whether it's a good fit
  • Reply to you and prepare a scope or quote
  • Deliver the engagement, if one follows

We do not sell, rent, or use your information for advertising. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Our legal basis for processing

Under Section 30 of Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, we process consultation request data on the basis of your consent (you choose to submit the form) and our legitimate interest in responding to business inquiries directed to us.

Who your data passes through

Two third-party services are involved in running this website, both acting as data processors on our behalf, not as independent controllers of your data:

  • Netlify, Inc. — hosts this website and receives consultation form submissions on our behalf.
  • Google Fonts — delivers the typefaces used on this site. Loading a font is a technical function of your browser contacting Google's servers directly, which involves your IP address being visible to Google as part of that request.

Where your data goes

Netlify's infrastructure is located outside Kenya. Submitting the consultation form therefore involves a cross-border transfer of your personal data. We rely on Netlify's standard data processing safeguards for this, consistent with Section 48 of Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, which governs the transfer of personal data outside Kenya.

How long we keep it

We keep consultation request details only as long as needed to respond to you and, if an engagement follows, for the life of that engagement plus a reasonable period afterward for our own records. If your inquiry doesn't lead to an engagement, we aim to delete it within 24 months of your last contact with us — sooner, if you ask us to.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Be informed of how your data is used (this page)
  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to how your data is processed
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these, email us using the address below. We'll respond within a reasonable time and without charge, save in exceptional cases the Act allows for.

If you're ever unsatisfied with how we've handled your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint directly with Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

Wider regulatory context

Sierra Leone does not yet have a dedicated data protection law in force — a bill has been through national validation but has not yet been enacted at the time of writing — so we apply the same standard set out in this policy across both our offices rather than a lower one where local law hasn't caught up.

Beyond Kenyan law specifically, this policy is also written in the spirit of the African Union's Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection (the Malabo Convention, in force continent-wide since June 2023) and internationally recognized data protection principles more broadly. We reference these as context for how we think about data handling, not as a claim of formal certification or registration under them.

Children's privacy

This website is directed at businesses, governments, and institutions, not children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices or the law changes. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version — we won't make material changes without updating it.

Contact us about your data

For any question about this policy, or to exercise your rights, email privacy@gtconsultancy.pro.

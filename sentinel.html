Sentinel: know where your lab stands before the auditor does

Sentinel is an online quality and compliance platform for medical, public-health, research and testing laboratories. It runs your gap analysis against the standard you are working towards, shows exactly what is missing, and tracks every fix until it is proven.

Start free Read the help centre See pricing

What Sentinel does

Gap analysis with a clear verdict

Score every audit question as Compliant, Minor gap, Major gap or Not applicable. Sentinel works out a weighted score for each section and suggests Approve, Needs review or Don't approve, flagging any failure in a critical clause.

A standards library in plain words

34 standards in 11 groups, each explained: who it is for, what evidence auditors ask for, and where to find the official text. Labs can add their own, such as a specific WHO standard or a national checklist.

From gap to proven fix

Turn any gap into a finding and a corrective action with a root cause, an owner, a deadline and an effectiveness check.

Evidence and audit trail

Keep evidence, staff competency and every change in one place, with a tamper-evident record of who did what and when.

How it works

  1. Register your lab

    Create an account. We set up your institution and your quality manager.

  2. Choose your standards

    Pick from the library or add your own.

  3. Run a gap analysis

    ISO 15189:2022 comes with a ready-made 116-question worksheet.

  4. Close the gaps

    Findings and corrective actions with owners and deadlines.

  5. Stay ready

    Evidence and history are ready for the next audit.
